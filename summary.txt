The burndown chart displays dates along the X-axis and story points along the Y-axis. It includes an ideal burndown line, 
which represents a steady, linear rate of progress based on the total story points and the total time available. 
By comparing the actual remaining story points to this ideal line, we can quickly assess our project’s progress. 
If the remaining points are above the ideal line, it indicates that we’re behind schedule; if they’re below the line, 
it means we’re ahead of schedule.
